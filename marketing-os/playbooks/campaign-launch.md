# Playbook: Campaign Launch (7-Day)

## Day 0: Intake + Planning
- Orchestrator validates brief and creates assignments.
- Messaging strategist publishes message hierarchy.

## Day 1-2: Asset Production
- SEO/content drafts landing and supporting content.
- Paid media prepares creative matrix and budgets.
- Lifecycle drafts nurture and activation flows.

## Day 3: Instrumentation QA
- Analytics agent validates events, UTMs, and dashboard views.
- Orchestrator resolves blockers and confirms launch criteria.

## Day 4: Launch
- Publish in sequence:
  1. Landing/offer page
  2. Paid campaigns
  3. Lifecycle journeys
  4. Organic distribution

## Day 5-6: Optimization Window
- Paid agent tunes bids and creative.
- Lifecycle agent checks engagement and adjusts triggers.
- Content agent amplifies top-performing assets.

## Day 7: Review
- Analytics agent delivers performance delta vs baseline.
- Orchestrator issues decision: scale, iterate, or stop.
